01 / ABOUT

From physical systems to working instruments.

Research shaped by an energy and power engineering background.

Measurement, computation, engineering

I am Wang Mingze (Huaqing), at the School of Energy and Power Engineering, Beihang University. My background is in Aerospace Intelligent Electric Propulsion; my doctoral research now centers on laser measurement instrumentation and advanced optical diagnostics.

I work across numerical models, embedded control, electronics and PCB design. The aim is to connect a physical question to a model, a measurable signal and an engineering system that can be tested.

My doctoral research now centers on laser measurement instrumentation and advanced optical diagnostics. Optical foundations and the specific technical branch are being developed through ongoing project work.
